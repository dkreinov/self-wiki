## Overview

Mr. Pink is an emergency physician and The Reservoir's most practically-minded member. He joined through [[Mr. Blonde]] during residency, when they discovered they had both grown up with the same edition of the same encyclopedia and both read it cover to cover. He has since become the group's anchor — the one who steers philosophical spirals back to something actionable.

## The Pragmatist

Mr. Pink's patience for theory is directly proportional to its proximity to something real. He follows [[Mr. White]]'s philosophical detours with interest up to the point where they detach from any human consequence, at which point he will say, flatly: "but what do you do with that?"

This is not anti-intellectualism. Mr. Pink reads more than anyone in the group and his recommendations are the ones most likely to change how you behave. He is simply impatient with abstraction that flatters the thinker without improving the world.

## Reading and Medicine

His reading splits between medicine and history, which he treats as the same discipline approached from different directions. He gave [[Mr. Blonde]] a copy of *The Emperor of All Maladies* and watched him spend two weeks in a state of quiet devastation. He considers *Meditations* by Marcus Aurelius the most practical book written before the twentieth century. He lent his copy to [[Mr. Silver]] expecting it back in a week; Mr. Silver kept it three months and returned it with more annotations than Mr. Pink had made.

## In the Group

Mr. Pink is the first to arrive and the first to notice when someone at the table is not okay. He asks direct questions about things others dance around. The group trusts his assessments because they know he spent years in a place where assessment had to be right. When the 2023 crisis hit, his activity in the group tripled — he became the de facto voice of calm when no one else could manage it.
