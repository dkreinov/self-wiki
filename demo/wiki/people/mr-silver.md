## Overview

Mr. Silver is a freelance illustrator whose clients range from children's books to technical documentation to editorial work. He joined The Reservoir through [[Mr. White]], who met him at a chess club exhibition — he was there to sketch the players, not play. He is the group's fastest thinker and most unpredictable conversationalist: he can be silent for forty minutes and then say the thing that reframes the entire evening.

## Sci-Fi as Philosophy

Mr. Silver reads science fiction as a form of philosophy — a way of isolating variables that reality keeps confounding. *The Left Hand of Darkness* by Ursula K. Le Guin is his most-cited book, a novel he has re-read six times and finds differently useful each time. He gave [[Mr. Blonde]] a copy expecting resistance; Mr. Blonde called him a week later and said it was the best book about history he had ever read, despite containing no history.

His recommendation of *Invisible Cities* by Calvino to [[Mr. White]] opened a three-month period in which Mr. White brought it up constantly. Mr. Silver considered this a success.

## The 2021 Peak

In 2021, Mr. Silver was the most active member of The Reservoir by a significant margin. The reasons were not announced. He was present in every conversation, responded to everything, initiated threads that ran for days. Then, without explanation, the activity dropped. By 2022 he was back to occasional appearances. By 2023 he was mostly quiet.

The group did not press him on it. [[Mr. Pink]] checked in privately. [[Mr. White]] sent a book recommendation without comment. The silence was treated as a form of communication that the group understood without being able to say what it meant.

## In the Group

Mr. Silver draws during conversations. He has sketchbooks full of The Reservoir — hands, expressions, table geometry. No one has seen these books in full. He occasionally shows a specific page to the specific person drawn on it, without comment.

His assessments of people are rarely stated directly. They emerge from a question asked, or an observation so oblique it takes a moment to land. [[Mr. Pink]] once said that Mr. Silver's compliments arrive on a three-second delay.
