## Overview

Mr. White is a backend engineer who has been part of The Reservoir since its founding. He works on distributed systems during the day and reads philosophy at night — a combination the group finds both impressive and exhausting. His conversational style runs Socratic: he rarely states a position, preferring questions that destabilize everyone else's.

## Conversational Role

Mr. White is The Reservoir's designated complicator. When [[Mr. Pink]] says something with confident authority, Mr. White asks the one question that makes it less certain. When [[Mr. Blonde]] delivers a verdict on history, Mr. White finds the counterexample. He is not a contrarian — he agrees when he agrees — but his default mode is interrogation rather than assertion.

The group has a running joke: if Mr. White goes quiet for more than ten minutes, someone will say "uh oh" and the table will brace.

## Reading and Thinking

Mr. White reads philosophy of mind, mathematics, and computer science as a single continuous subject. His three most-pressed books — *Gödel, Escher, Bach*, *The Trial*, and *Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance* — all circle the same question: what does it mean to think inside a system you cannot fully see? He presses them on others with the enthusiasm of someone who believes the answer is in there somewhere, if only someone would read carefully enough.

## On Staying

Of all The Reservoir's members, Mr. White is the one whose attachment to the country has loosened most visibly over the years. He does not announce this. It surfaces in what he does not say — the arguments he no longer makes, the future plans that have quietly become hypothetical. When asked directly, he gives a careful answer about conditions and thresholds. The group reads between the lines.
