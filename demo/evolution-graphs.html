The Reservoir — Group Evolution 2015–2025

Ten years of a WhatsApp group. How did each person change? All data sourced from ~500,000 messages analyzed by an LLM. Estimated values noted.

1 — Who Was Speaking, and When

Normalized activity score (1–10) per person per year. Key group events annotated. Notice Mr. Silver's dramatic 2021 peak and near-silence after; Mr. Pink's war-era explosion; Mr. Blonde's rage-quit cycles.

* Activity normalized: 10 = approx. 4500+ messages/year; 1 = near-absent. Others estimated from narrative.

2 — Political Compass: Early (2015–18) → Late (2023–25)

Where each person stood politically at group founding, and where they stand now. X-axis: economic left–right. Y-axis: social liberal–conservative. Arrows show direction of change. The dot is the current position.

* Mr. Blonde's arrow is the largest — from self-described left-wing voter to right-leaning nationalist. Mr. Silver drifted right through anti-establishment positions. All others largely stable.

3 — Hometown Attachment Score Over Time

Commitment to staying in the home country (0 = already abroad and settled there, 10 = deeply rooted). Emigration events are marked. The 2023 crisis deepened attachment for some (Mr. Brown, Mr. Blonde, Mr. Pink) and loosened it for others (Mr. Green, Mr. White).

* Emigration events: Mr. Blue ~2017, Mr. Teal ~2017, Mr. Gold ~2018. Mr. Blonde turned down an overseas offer ~2018. Mr. White's stated reason for staying: aging parents.

4 — What the Group Was Talking About, by Era

Estimated share of group discussion across 8 topic categories, split by four eras. The dominance of crisis/conflict content post-2023 is striking — it crowded out nearly everything else.

5 — Interest Profile: Early vs. Late

How each person's engagement across 6 dimensions shifted between the founding era and the crisis era. Select a person to view their radar. Each dimension scored 0–10 based on message content analysis.

6 — Personality Archetypes: The Founding Eight

Six dimensions of conversational personality for the founding members (2015 cohort). Each chart shows a single person's profile: how analytical, provocative, humorous, politically engaged, emotionally volatile, and technically expert they are in the group.

* Dimensions: Analytical (data-driven reasoning), Provocateur (how much they stir debate), Humor (comedic contribution), Political (political engagement intensity), Emotional (range and expressiveness), Technical (domain expertise shared).
